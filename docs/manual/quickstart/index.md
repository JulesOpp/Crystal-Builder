# Quickstart Guide

This chapter gets Crystal Builder installed and running, and takes you through a first build from a net and two building blocks to a relaxed, symmetrised framework.  By its end you know where everything lives in the window and what to do when something refuses.

:::{note}
This chapter is being written.  Until it is, the generated reference
in {ref}`the appendix <reference-appendix>` describes every command,
module and setting the application offers.
:::

## What this chapter will cover

- About Crystal Builder
- Installation: the packaged application, a source install and its extras, and the external programs ({ref}`Zeo++ <mod-zeopp>`, {ref}`DFTB+ <mod-dftb>`, xTB) and where Preferences points at them
- The window: toolbar, {doc}`panels </reference/panels>`, workspaces and the workspace chooser
- Your first crystal build: the {ref}`MOF builder <mod-mof-build>` on **acs**, a {ref}`force field <mod-forcefield-optimise>` relaxation, *Find symmetry*, and drawing the net
- General recommendations
- Troubleshooting
